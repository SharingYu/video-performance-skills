## Task

Make one six-group event card for reconstructing the performance in a different setting. Preserve the visible process that makes the interaction readable; furniture and decoration may change.

Keep three layers separate:

- **Supplied record:** the text above, including what it does not specify.
- **Interpretation:** for example, a reach is interrupted by recognition, followed by a cover activity. This is a director's reading, not a proven internal state.
- **New design:** any framing or staging you propose to make those relationships legible.

Do not infer gaze from head direction, intent from a single pose, or Nori's emotion from stillness. A composed response can be carried by looking at the changed action and moving the dish; extra smiles or head shakes are not required.
